# IMAGE_PROMPTS.md

**These are AI-generation reference prompts only. Images generated from these prompts are not certified samples and must not be presented as such.**
Images must be placed in `public/images/` using the exact filename listed.
All real supplier or sample photographs take precedence over generated images.

Notation: aspect ratio and pixel dimensions are targets for the generation tool.
- Hero: 3:2 — 1200 × 800 px
- Category tile: 4:3 — 800 × 600 px (or 1600 × 1200 px for higher-res)
- Product (1:1): 800 × 800 px

Negative prompt (apply to every image): **no text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no extra objects in frame, no lens distortion, no motion blur, no digital artefacts.**
