# IMAGE_PROMPTS.md

> **All images generated from these prompts are illustrative AI imagery, not certified samples.**
> Do not present generated images as product samples, certified test results, or supplier photographs.
> Replace with real supplier or sample photographs wherever available — they always take precedence.

---

## Common style prefix (apply to every prompt below)

> Photorealistic studio product photograph. Pure white (#FFFFFF) seamless paper backdrop, no shadows on the backdrop itself. Soft diffused top-left studio light (45° above, 30° left), one soft fill light from right. Soft natural contact shadow directly under the object, no cast shadows. Subject fully contained within the central 75% of the frame with clear white margin on all sides. No text, no labels, no logos, no watermarks, no extra objects, no props. Tack-sharp focus, realistic depth of field, no motion blur, no lens distortion.

## Common negative prompt (copy to every generation tool)

> text, labels, logos, watermarks, branding, extra objects in frame, background gradients, coloured backgrounds, lens distortion, motion blur, over-sharpened, HDR, painted look, illustration, render, 3D, cartoon, sketch, human hands, fingers, faces, identifiable people
